Harlequins

Lineout Strike

Open carry // Make them chase

Pull the defence to the open side with a hard carry — then strike back at the short side they've abandoned.

  • ▸ 9 passes to 10; 10 passes to the 6 & 8 pod, who carry open to make the defence chase.
  • ▸ The forward at the back of the lineout hits the breakdown.
  • ▸ Any non-lifters work around the corner.
  • ▸ The lifting pod stays — 10 is the option off it.
  • ▸ The hooker works open after his throw.
  • ▸ 11, 15 & 12 attack the blindside.
02

Bath

Lineout Strike

Against the grain // Then backs move

Carry back against the grain to bunch the defence and secure it tight — then swing the ball into a backs move, everyone working open.

  • ▸ 9 passes straight to the 8 & 6 pod, who carry back against the grain to make the defence bunch.
  • ▸ The forward at the back of the lineout hits the ruck.
  • ▸ The lifting pod & hooker work around the corner, carrying tight to the breakdown.
  • ▸ The backs then work open into a backs move.
03

Toulouse

Phase Strike // Bingo

Bingo out to the far side // Then back across

Work the ball out to the far side with the Bingo shape — forwards running hard, ball out the back — then wash it straight back across the field with a second Bingo wave to finish on the short side.

  • ▸ Wave 1 — ball works left to right out to the far side. Forwards run hard lines up and back against the ball, and it's played out the back at each link: 9, 10, F, 12, F, 13, 14, 15.
  • ▸ 14, 15 & the hooker hit the wide breakdown (BD 1) on the far side.
  • ▸ 9 scoots off BD 1, taking the non-lifting pod (NLP) option through to breakdown 2, just back in from the touchline.
  • ▸ Wave 2 — the same Bingo shape washes back right to left: 9, 10, F, 12, F, 13, with 12 & 13 sitting deep. 11 finishes on the left with a forward out in support.
9 — Feeds Wave 1, then scoots off BD 1 through the NLP to BD 2 to launch Wave 2.
10 — First receiver in both waves; plays out the back off the forward's hard line.
Forwards (F) — Run hard lines up and back against the ball to fix defenders; ball goes out the back of them.
12 — Out-the-back receiver; sits deep in Wave 2.
13 — Out-the-back receiver; sits deep in Wave 2 and feeds 11.
14, 15 & 2 — 14 & 15 carry Wave 1 to the far side; they and the hooker hit BD 1.
11 — Finishes Wave 2 on the left, with a forward out in support.
04

La Rochelle

Blindside Strike

Sell it open // Strike back blind

Crash the middle and take the whole pack open to drag their forwards across — then, while 12, 13 & 11 quietly reload on the blind, swing it straight back to catch them sleeping on the short side.

  • ▸ Pure dummy maul. The catcher shows the maul, then a forward runs a bingo line and the ball is played out the back to 9.
  • ▸ 9 short to 11, who crashes up the middle. 12 & 13 hit that breakdown (Ruck 1).
  • ▸ Forwards carry off 9 and work open to Ruck 2 — the sell that drags their forwards across and buys the reload time.
  • ▸ 10 drops in under Ruck 2 and takes it off 9 on the blind, with 12 · 13 · 11 reloaded on the blind side — no forwards there.
  • ▸ They play what's in front and attack up the short side — catching the opposition forwards sleeping.

The Rec

One-Phase Strike

Sweep it across // Out the back twice

One phase off the same dummy-maul set-up — two bingo lines hold the fringe defenders while the ball is played out the back each time, sweeping across to a three-way finish.

  • ▸ Dummy maul. A forward breaks off the maul and passes to 10.
  • ▸ A forward runs a bingo line off 10; 10 plays out the back to 9, who's folded around to the bottom of 10.
  • ▸ 13 runs a bingo line off 9; 9 plays out the back to 12 (12 & 11 folded around the corner).
  • ▸ 14 & 15 hold wide throughout.
11 — on a shoulder ball.
15 — on a flat pass.
