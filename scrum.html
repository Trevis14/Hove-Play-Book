Chiefs

Scrum Strike

Miss 10 // Square 12 // Out the back of 13

9 misses 10 straight to 12, who stays square and takes it to the gain line. 10 folds in behind, comes out the back of 13's hard line, and 14 arrives on his outside for the shoulder ball.

  • ▸ 9 gives a straight pass to 12.
  • ▸ 10 stands at first receiver and drops in behind 12.
  • ▸ 12 stays square with a little kick out, taking the ball to the gain line.
  • ▸ 13 comes on a hard line off 12.
  • ▸ 10 comes out the back of 13.
  • ▸ 11 shadows 10 and pops up on the outside for a shoulder ball.
  • ▸ 15 is the across-the-face option; 11 can hit wide.
  • ▸ 14 holds the width.
When to call it — This can be played against any style of defence, but we have to realise when to give ourselves extra time on the ball if they are blitzing.
Where — Anywhere on the pitch, including open play. Playable off a left- or right-hand scrum.
Then… — If we hit up the middle we play a 9K, and if we hit an edge we play a 9K back in field.
02

Waratahs

Scrum Strike

Chiefs set-up // 12 strikes 11 through the seam

The same picture as Chiefs, but the ball leaves a phase earlier and the strike runner changes. 10 still slides out the back, only now as a decoy — 12 holds the ball and puts 11 through the seam that opens between where 13 starts and where 10 finishes.

  • ▸ 9 gives a straight pass to 12, missing 10.
  • ▸ 12 stays square to the gain line and makes the pass.
  • ▸ 13 comes on a hard line off 12 — forward first, then back in.
  • ▸ 10 slides out the back and away towards 13's outside, taking a defender with him — he is the across-the-face option.
  • ▸ 11 runs the seam between where 13 starts and where 10 ends up.
  • ▸ 14 holds the width, wide for the cross-field kick.
When to call it — Any style of defence — we just adjust our depth to fit.
Where — Anywhere in the second and third thirds of the pitch.
Then… — If we hit up the middle, forwards work around the corner for a 9K. If we hit an edge, forwards work back in field for a 9K.
03

Crusaders

Scrum Strike

13 pulls the winger out // 14 hits the gap

13 runs away from the ball to drag the defending winger with him, and 14 comes back the other way into the channel 13 has just emptied. 11 and 15 give 12 two closer options if the far side is covered.

  • ▸ 9 passes to 10.
  • ▸ 10 passes to 12.
  • ▸ 13 runs a wide line, going outside the defending winger.
  • ▸ 11 runs a short shoulder ball off 12.
  • ▸ 15 holds his line just outside 11.
  • ▸ 14 runs hard into the gap 13 has left open.
When to call it — Any style of defence — but we must adjust our depth if they blitz, and be aware of the intercept.
Where — Second and third thirds of the pitch.
Then… — The forwards always play a 9K around the corner and then a 9K back in field, allowing the backs time to get set to play back across the pitch.

Moana

Scrum Strike // Blindside

8 picks blind // Short side only

We aim to get the scrum up on the right side. 8 picks blind fixing the flanker, 9 goes on the outside fixing the next defender, and then you have 15 and 14.

  • ▸ 8 picks and goes blind, fixing the 6, and pops to 9.
  • ▸ 9 gets the pop off 8 and has 15 and 14 as options.
  • ▸ 15 starts wide, in the channel between 9 and 14, and runs outside 9.
  • ▸ 14 holds flat and on the touchline.
  • ▸ 12 runs an in-line off 10.
  • ▸ 13 runs an in-line off 10.
  • ▸ 11 always stretches the defence.
When to call it — If we have a scrum on the right-hand side of the pitch.
Where — Any third of the pitch.
Then… — Forwards play a quick 9K and we look to attack the other side of the pitch, as the defence will be on the back foot.

Brumbies

Scrum Strike

9 off the 8 // Centres in, 10 out the back

9 takes the pop off 8 with three pictures in front of him — crash 12, crash 13, or hit 10. Both centres run in off him, so 10 gets the ball out the back of a defence that has already turned.

  • ▸ 9 gets the pop off 8 and picks either a crash on 12 or 13, or hits 10.
  • ▸ 10 runs a line out the back of the centres, with 11 as an inside option and 15 as the wide option.
  • ▸ 12 runs an out-to-in line off 9.
  • ▸ 13 runs an out-to-in line off 9.
  • ▸ 15 runs a wide line expecting the ball from 10.
  • ▸ 11 runs a line holding on the inside shoulder of 10.
  • ▸ 14 holds the wide channel and reacts to the play — possible cross-field option.
When to call it — If the defence is tight and has a hard drift.
Where — Anywhere on the pitch — but in the first third, only hit up the 12 and 13 to set a platform.
Then… — In the first third we set up to exit. In the other two thirds, if we hit the middle the forwards work around the corner for a 9K; if we hit the edge the forwards take a 9K back in field.

Highlanders

Scrum Strike

Switch in the middle // 15 out the back

A switch between 10 and 12 turns the defence inward, 13 and 11 attack the seams either side of it, and 15 arrives out the back with the ball already past the first line of drift.

  • ▸ 10 runs a switch with 12, then looks for the best option.
  • ▸ 12 runs the switch line off 10.
  • ▸ 13 runs an out-to-in line, tight to 10.
  • ▸ 15 runs an out-the-back line, bouncing behind 13.
  • ▸ 11 runs a J line into the gap created between 10 and 12.
  • ▸ 14 holds the wide channel and reacts to the play — possible cross-field option.
When to call it — The 12 switch option and the 13 hard line will either hold the drift, opening space on the outside, or the gap will open up because they have drifted too hard.
Where — Middle third of the pitch.
Then… — If we hit the middle channel, forwards work around the corner for a 9K. If we hit an edge, forwards work for a 9K back in field.
07

Hurricanes

Scrum Strike

Out the back twice // 11 then 15

Two out-the-backs in a row. 12 and 13 run hard lines that have to be respected, the ball goes behind each of them in turn, and by the time 14 gets it the defence has been beaten twice inside.

  • ▸ 12 runs a hard line off 10.
  • ▸ 10 plays 11 out the back of 12.
  • ▸ 13 runs a hard line off 11.
  • ▸ 11 plays 15 out the back of 13.
  • ▸ 14 holds his width, playing off 15.
  • ▸ 12 and 13 are to always run these lines.
  • ▸ If they read it — cancel and hit 12 or 13 up, or go out the back of them to 15. A cross-field kick to 11 is the other out.
When to call it — If the defence is either a hard drift or they hard press.
Where — Middle third, and from a scrum on either edge.
Then… — If we hit up the middle, forwards work around the corner for a 9K. If we hit an edge, forwards set for a 9K back in field.
08

Blues

Scrum Strike // Two-way

Same picture // Goes either way

One shape, two directions. Both centres run in-lines that fix the flanker, the 9 and the 8, and 10 goes early behind whichever one the call sends him to.

  • ▸ 9 — going right, scoots and fixes the 7. Going left, gets the pop off the floor from 8.
  • ▸ 10 has to go early, receiving the ball behind either 12 or 13.
  • ▸ 12 runs an in-line, fixing the flanker, 9 and 8.
  • ▸ 13 runs an in-line, fixing the flanker, 9 and 8.
  • ▸ 15 has to set off slightly earlier than 10, picking his support line either narrow or wide.
  • ▸ 11 holds the width.
  • ▸ 14 holds the width.
  • ▸ 9 follows his pass — he is always an inside option for 10.
When to call it — If we have a scrum in the middle of the pitch.
Where — Second and third thirds only.
Then… — If we hit the middle, forwards work open with a 9K. If we hit an edge, forwards work for a 9K back inside.
